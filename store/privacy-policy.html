Privacy Policy — Group Post Dedup

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Group Post Dedup (“the extension”) hides or collapses repeated posts that the same person publishes to several groups on facebook.com. This policy explains what it does with data.

Summary

  • The extension works entirely inside your browser.
  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. It has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no remote code.
  • It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc.

What the extension reads

While you browse facebook.com, the extension reads, from the page already loaded in your browser, for each post in your feed: the post text, the author name and identifier, the group name and identifier, and the post identifier. This is used only to decide whether two posts are the same content. The data is processed in memory and is not transmitted anywhere.

What the extension stores on your device

  1. Settings (on/off, collapse or hide, similarity threshold, “same author only”, “remember seen posts”), saved with chrome.storage.sync. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync enabled, Chrome itself may sync these settings between your devices under your Google account; the developer has no access to them.
  2. Remembered posts (optional, on by default), saved with chrome.storage.local for at most 24 hours and at most 500 entries. Each entry contains a one-way hash of the normalised post text, the post and author/group identifiers, the author and group display names, and a timestamp. The original post text is not stored. You can turn this off or delete it at any time from the extension popup (“Xóa dữ liệu đã nhớ”), or by removing the extension.
  3. Temporary tab bookkeeping, saved with chrome.storage.session (kept in memory, cleared when the browser closes). Only when you use the “block” button on a comment and Facebook’s comment menu has no block option, the extension remembers the numeric id of the background tab it opened and of the tab you clicked in, so it can report the result back. It contains no personal data and is deleted when that tab closes.

Diagnostic report

The popup has a “Chẩn đoán” button. When you click it, the extension builds a short report (page address, settings, element counts and, for the first 12 posts, identifiers, names and the first 40 characters of the text) and copies it to your clipboard so that you can paste it where you choose. Nothing is sent automatically.

Permissions

  • Access to *.facebook.com: required to read the feed and to collapse duplicate posts. The extension does not run on any other site.
  • storage: required to save the settings and remembered posts described above.

Data sharing and sale

The extension does not sell, share, or transfer user data to anyone, does not use data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose (hiding duplicate posts), and does not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13.
